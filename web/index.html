What is ILU?

ILU — I Listen to You is a digital wellbeing companion designed to give you a gentle space to pause, reflect, and appreciate the little things that often go unnoticed.

Through a simple gratitude-based experience, ILU invites users to recognize three good things from their day and reflect on them.

Let's Talk About Mental Health

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social wellbeing. It affects how we think, feel, and act as we cope with life. It also helps determine how we handle stress, relate to others, and make choices.

According to the WHO, depression is a common mental disorder. It is more than just a bout of sadness. It involves a persistent low mood and a loss of pleasure or interest in activities, lasting for at least two weeks.

You should consider seeking help when you experience persistent symptoms that affect your daily life, relationships, or ability to function. Reaching out to a trusted adult or a mental health professional is a courageous first step.

Listen to them without judgment, be present, and offer a safe space for them to share. Encourage them gently to seek professional support if they are struggling, and remind them that they are not alone.

Get to Know ILU

🌻 Why we created ILU
We wanted to build a safe, non-judgmental space where people can pause and breathe. ILU was designed recognizing that sometimes the best support is just someone—or something—that listens.
💭 What happens inside ILU
Inside ILU, you'll find gentle prompts, a listening ear, and a space to record your thoughts. There's no pressure to feel a certain way—just an invitation to be present.
✨ Why gratitude matters
Practicing gratitude doesn't mean ignoring the hard things. It's about training our minds to also notice the small sparks of light in our day, which can gently shift our perspective over time.
